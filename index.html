How a project runs

From first message to handover, with no guesswork about price or progress.

  1. Send the idea

    Message me on Discord with what you want. A rough idea is enough to start.

  2. Agree scope and price

    I confirm deliverables, price and timeline. The quote is fixed before work starts.

  3. Build with updates

    You get progress updates as the work comes together. Bigger builds arrive in milestones.

  4. Handover and fixes

    The finished work is handed over, with fixes for bugs in what I delivered.

Questions

Anything else, ask on Discord.

How is the price decided?

The rates above are starting points. Once we agree what will be delivered, I give you a fixed quote and timeline before any work starts.

Can you work inside my existing game?

Yes. The feature package is built for existing games: features are integrated into your place and tested there.

Will I see progress during the build?

Yes. You get progress updates as the work comes together, and bigger builds are delivered in milestones.

What if something breaks after delivery?

Bugs in the work I delivered get fixed. New features or changes to the agreed scope are quoted separately.

Do you take animation and building work?

Yes. Animation is quoted from the number of animations and building from the size of the build. Ask on Discord for examples.
