// FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long will my project take?+

Feature packages, systems builds and prototypes are scheduled around their scope and integration requirements. You receive a realistic timeline before work begins.

What does the $600 starting price cover?+

The base package is for two small, clearly defined features. A complex combat system or another substantial mechanic may need a larger scope. We agree exactly what is included, how it connects to your existing game, and the final price before work begins.

How does payment work?+

Payment structure is agreed with the scope. Larger work can be split into clear milestones so delivery and payment stay aligned.

What happens after delivery?+

You receive the agreed files and ownership. I also provide practical support for bugs caused by the delivered work.

Can you work inside an existing game?+

Yes. I can improve or extend existing systems after reviewing the current architecture and confirming a safe scope.

How do I get started?+

Send a short brief using the form below or email me directly. Your game link and deadline are optional, and “Not sure yet” is fine for the budget. I’ll reply to the contact details you provide.
