FAQ

How long will my project take?

It depends on scope and how much it has to connect to. You get a realistic timeline before work begins.

What does the $600 starting price cover?

Two small, clearly defined features. A complex combat system or another substantial mechanic may need a larger scope. We agree exactly what's included and the final price before work begins.

How does payment work?

It's agreed with the scope. Larger work can be split into milestones so delivery and payment stay aligned.

What happens after delivery?

You receive the agreed files and ownership. I also fix bugs caused by the delivered work.

Can you work inside an existing game?

Yes. I review the current architecture first and confirm a safe scope.
